Project Overview

This project focuses on assembling a high-magnification compound microscope from accessible optical components. By coupling a 45x microscope objective lens with a laser light focusing lens and a Foldscope lens system, the assembly yields clear 180x optical magnification. Speaker magnets serve as a heavy, adjustable magnetic base to maintain structural alignment and enable steady sample observation.

Key Specifications & Optics

  • Compound Magnification: Achieve clear ~180x target magnification for observing cellular structures and microscopic details.
  • 45x Objective Lens: Primary optical element providing high resolving power and sharp focal acquisition.
  • Sub-Stage Focusing Lens: Repurposed small-aperture laser diode lens to condense and collimate illumination rays.
  • Foldscope Lens Coupling: Micro-lens integration extending effective focal length and compound multiplication.
  • Magnetic Base Assembly:4 Heavy speaker magnets used for rigid mounting, fine stage levelling, and quick component adjustment.

Assembly & Optical Alignment

    1. Primary Optical Stacking: Aligned the 45x objective lens inline with the Foldscope lens module to establish high primary multiplication. 2. Condenser Lens Integration: Positioned the laser pointer lens below the sample stage to concentrate incoming light across the focal plane. 3. Magnetic Base Assembly: Mounted the structural column onto speaker magnets to ensure low vibration and easy magnetic coupling. 4. Focal Testing & Observation: Tested various biological and material slides to verify image clarity and edge-to-edge resolution at 180x.

Outcome & Reflection

The composite system provided impressive optical magnification and resolution while keeping construction simple and affordable. The speaker magnet base proved extremely effective for maintaining mechanical stability during fine focus adjustments.
