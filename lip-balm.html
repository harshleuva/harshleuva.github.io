DIY Organic Lip Balm

Ratio 1 Part Wax : 2.5–3 Parts Oil
Key Action Moisturize & Occlusive Shield
Antioxidant Vitamin E Oil
Status Successfully Tested

What is Lip Balm?

Lip balm is a hydrating, wax-like substance applied to the lips to moisturize, soothe, and protect them. It creates a physical shield that locks in hydration, preventing the delicate skin of the lips from drying out due to cold weather, dry air, or sun exposure.

Materials Required

  • 1 part Beeswax
  • 2.5 to 3 parts Fractionated Coconut Oil
  • 1–2 drops Vitamin E Oil (or 1 capsule per container)
  • Essential Oil (for natural fragrance)
  • Small storage containers / dibbi
  • Double-boiler setup (water, containers, induction/burner)

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Combine 1 part beeswax and 2.5 to 3 parts fractionated coconut oil in a container.
  2. Heat slowly using a double boiling setup until the beeswax melts completely.
  3. Remove from heat source and quickly stir in Vitamin E oil and desired essential oil fragrance.
  4. Pour the liquid mixture quickly into small storage containers before solidification begins.

The Simple Science Behind It

1. Beeswax as an Occlusive Barrier

Beeswax contains natural fatty acids and long-chain alcohols that melt at around 62–65°C. When applied to lips, it solidifies into a breathable, protective hydrophobic barrier that seals in moisture and prevents transepidermal water loss.

2. Coconut Oil for Deep Emollience

Coconut oil is rich in medium-chain fatty acids (like lauric acid) that penetrate the outer layer of skin. While beeswax seals the surface, coconut oil softens the skin matrix and restores lost lipid moisture.

3. Vitamin E & Antioxidant Protection

Vitamin E serves a dual purpose: it neutralizes free radical damage on dry, cracked lips while acting as a natural preservative that prevents the plant oils from going rancid over time.
