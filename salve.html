The Science Behind Rapid Pain Relief

1. TRPM8 & TRPV1 Nociceptor Gate Control

Menthol from Peppermint/Spearmint oils activates TRPM8 (cold-sensing) cutaneous receptors, while Camphor targets TRPV1 heat channels. This dual thermal excitation sends rapid inhibitory signals to the spinal cord, blocking forehead pain transmission (Gate Control Theory of Pain) in under 5 minutes.

2. Controlled Vapor Pressure (No Eye Burning)

Commercial rub balms rely on ultra-high concentrations of volatile synthetic menthol or methyl salicylate, which evaporate rapidly and irritate ocular mucous membranes. By trapping essential oils in a dense 62% coconut oil and 22% beeswax lipid matrix, vapor release is slowed down to prevent eye stinging while maintaining local dermal absorption.

3. Synergistic Transdermal Carrier System

Fractionated medium-chain fatty acids in coconut oil act as natural skin penetration enhancers, rapidly driving active terpenes through the stratum corneum into superficial cranial microcapillaries for localized vasodilation and muscle tension relief.
