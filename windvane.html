The Journey & Realization

1. The Misconception Almost Everyone Has

Ask anyone which way a wind vane points, and almost everyone immediately says: "In the direction the wind is going." It sounds completely logical! But in reality, it points directly into the coming wind (where the wind originates). Clearing this simple, deep-rooted misconception was the whole spark behind this activity.

2. Building it Hands-On at Ratanpur Govt School

I visited the government school in Ratanpur (near GIFT City) and spent the day with kids from classes 6th, 7th, and 8th. Instead of just showing slides or reading textbook definitions, we grabbed everyday materials—paper, straws, and pins—and built working wind vanes together.

3. The "Aha!" Moment Outdoors

When the kids took their models outside into the open breeze, they saw it live: the bigger tail fin catches more wind push, swinging away, which automatically turns the sharp arrowhead around to face right into the incoming breeze. Seeing their faces light up when they realized why the arrow behaves that way was the best part of the workshop.

4. Taking the Lesson Online with CCL IITGN

To share this simple yet effective teaching method with a bigger audience, we hosted an online session on the YouTube channel of Center for Creative Learning (CCL), IIT Gandhinagar. Demonstrating these low-cost hands-on models live helped educators and students everywhere see how fun science can be when learned by building.
