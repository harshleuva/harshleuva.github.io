Project Overview

This project focuses on constructing a functional Tesla turbine to study boundary layer effect drag propulsion rather than conventional bladed rotors. Paired with a secondary resonant Tesla coil setup, the experiment explores energy transfer, mechanical conversion, and high-frequency high-voltage physics in a benchtop lab environment.

Key Specifications & Features

  • Rotor Material: Smooth acrylic discs stacked with precise spacing to optimize fluid boundary interaction.
  • Drive Mechanism: Compressed fluid/air inlet driving rotation without mechanical blades.
  • Resonant Secondary: Custom hand-wound coil tuned for high-frequency output spark discharge.
  • Housing: Compact custom assembly designed for benchtop stability and visual tracking.

Build & Testing Methodology

    1. Design & Component Sourcing: CAD layout for disc spacing and casing tolerances. 2. Mechanical Fabrication: Precision cutting and shaft mounting to maintain low frictional vibration. 3. Circuit Assembly & Tuning: Primary/secondary winding alignment and spark gap spacing. 4. Bench Testing & Data Collection: Air pressure testing to measure RPM stability and voltage discharge ranges.
