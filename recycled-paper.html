Recycled Paper & Disposable Deskmat

A hands-on paper-making experiment using newspaper pulp, cornstarch binding agent, and customizable mechanical pressing.

Base Pulp Newspaper Mesh
Binder Cornstarch Matrix
Dual Function Writable Paper / Deskmat
Status Completed

Toolset & Raw Materials

  • Wooden Frame with fine Wire Mesh (Deckle/Mould)
  • Shredded Newspaper Pulp (Pulp Mesh)
  • Cornstarch (Natural Binding Agent)
  • Water for pulping
  • Sunlight Drying Surface

Fabrication Process

  1. Prepare newspaper pulp and mix with cornstarch binder solution.
  2. Scoop and distribute pulp evenly over the wooden wire mesh frame.
  3. Drain excess water and place in direct sunlight for sun drying.
  4. Post-drying treatment: Apply heavy mechanical pressure to flatten for writing, or leave one side unpressed for a cushioned finish.

Experimental Sample

The Science Behind the Material Dynamics

1. Hydrogen Bonding & Fiber Interlocking

Newspaper pulp consists of cellulose fibers. When soaked in water, these fibers swell and tangle. As water evaporates during sun drying, hydrogen bonds form between adjacent hydroxyl groups (-OH) on neighboring cellulose molecules, locking the fibers into a cohesive matrix.

2. Role of Cornstarch (Sizing Agent)

Cornstarch acts as a natural sizing binder. Gelatinized starch molecules fill micro-voids between cellulose fibers, increasing surface smoothness, structural stiffness, and preventing ink from feathering (bleeding through) when writing.

3. Compression Mechanics & Dual Utility

Hard-Pressed State: Mechanical compression flattens air pockets, increases fiber density, and aligns surface cellulose for smooth pen writing.
Unpressed / Soft State: Leaving one side unpressed retains microscopic air gaps within the fibrous mesh. This lower density structure provides impact absorption, making it an ideal disposable, scratch-resistant desk cushion for laptops, phones, and watches.😀
